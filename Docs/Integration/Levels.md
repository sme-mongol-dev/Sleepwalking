# Level Design → Game Mechanics and Visual

## Grid and tile contract

The seven prefabs at `Assets/SleepwalkingLevels/Prefabs/L##_DreamGeometry.prefab` are ordered L01–L07 dreams. Instantiate one at world origin without additional scaling. The root XY scale is `(32,32)`, Z is `1`: one local cell occupies 32×32 Unity world units. The world grid begins at `(0,0)`. Local interval `[a,b)` covers cells `a` through `b-1`. Theme changes preserve the same geometry and colliders; distant backgrounds have no collider.

## Tutorial contract

Two editable prefabs precede the seven main dreams: `Assets/SleepwalkingLevels/Prefabs/Tutorial01_DreamGeometry.prefab` and `Tutorial02_DreamGeometry.prefab`. Their `SleepwalkingLevelDefinition.LevelIndex` values are `-2` and `-1`. They use the same 32-unit root scale, `PlayerStart`, `BedExit`, and `Geometry/Hazards/Mechanisms/Markers/Readability` structure as the main dreams. Place world-space instruction signs under `Readability/TutorialHints`; the game leaves their authored sprite colors intact when applying a dream style.

`SleepwalkingGame.TutorialPrefabs[0..1]` references those prefabs separately from `LevelPrefabs[0..6]`. Tutorial stage 0 loads Tutorial1 with dash blocked in `SleepwalkingPlayer`; stage 1 loads Tutorial2 with dash enabled. Completing either tutorial wakes the player into the room and advances only the tutorial stage. Completing Tutorial2 makes L01 next. `CompletedDreams` counts only main dreams, so L01–L07 keep their existing indices, first-cycle style order and repeating seven-level cycle. Starting a new game resets the tutorial stage. Tutorial1 teaches A/D running, Space jumping and wall grab/jump; Tutorial2 teaches Shift dash.

`Docs/QA/tutorial-progression-results.xml` passed 1/1 in PlayMode on the current scene: the two tutorials and L01 loaded in order with a room wake between them, Shift was blocked in Tutorial1 and enabled in Tutorial2, L07 looped to L01 without tutorials, and New Game reset tutorial stage. This test invokes level completion directly; it does not prove the physical tutorial routes or visual readability of the instruction signs.

`Geometry/CaveShell`, static platforms and foundations form one occupied-cell matrix. Solid cells occupy integer local cell coordinates, and adjacent pieces must have neither overlapping cells nor gaps. `SleepwalkingTileSurface.Solid` refers to each object's `Collider2D`; saved tile surfaces use `PolygonCollider2D`. Build the visible surface from the combined occupancy of all static solids, including neighbours belonging to a different GameObject. The static physical contour trims exposed outer corners by up to four source pixels and tile-17 inner corners by two pixels. Shared joins and straight top, bottom and side contact faces stay on the 32-unit grid. Timed and moving platforms start on this same grid but are separate rectangular polygon islands so they can move or disappear with their colliders. All four styles use the same physical contour. Each occupied cell renders one uncompressed, point-filtered 32×32 source tile; open cells render no solid tile and have no solid collider.

For this shaped-collider revision, `Tools/check-colliders.cjs` passes all nine saved prefabs and `Tools/validate-prefabs.cjs` passes the seven dream prefabs. `Docs/QA/tile-shape-cave-results.xml` passed 1/1 for rendered tile coverage against the static collision bounds; `tile-shape-L05-results.xml` passed 2/2 for the timed staircase. `tile-shape-L07-results.xml` failed 0/2: both profiles landed on `WallGrabLedge`, then crossed the first button without a recorded contact and died at the laser. That physical route remains unresolved; older route passes below predate this collider revision.

A saw's rotation axis sits on its support block's top edge, with its lower half rendered behind that block. No object-local border may appear where occupied cells touch. Render dreams directly at screen resolution from the original textures, without the room's low-resolution RenderTexture pass.

## Gameplay attachment

The prefab root carries `SleepwalkingLevelDefinition` with its start and exit markers. `Geometry`, `Hazards`, `Mechanisms`, `Markers`, and `Readability` remain independently editable. `SleepwalkingLevelFeature` supplies `FeatureId`, `Kind`, `LinkedFeatureId`, `ActiveSeconds`, `MoveOffset`, `CycleSeconds`, and `PhaseSeconds`. L04's button targets `L04_Laser`; L07 buttons A and B target `L07_Laser` and `L07_Saw`. Laser base and beam are separately lethal. A linked button temporarily hides and disables only the beam; the emitter cone remains visible and lethal. The beam returns when the timer expires (`Docs/QA/laser-emitter-separation-results.xml`, PlayMode 1/1). L05 timed pads are active for 5.5 s in a 7 s cycle, with phases 0/0.3/0/0.3/0 and a 1.5 s off interval. Retry restores platforms, traps and player start. Open gaps have visible spikes on solid foundations above bedrock beginning at Y=-4; there is no invisible kill plane. Matching 3D colliders belong on `Collision3D` children; gameplay collision is 2D.

The intended player collider is 0.55×1 local cells (17.6×32 world units). Player velocities, gravity, contact probes, spawn offsets, hazard art, beam width and camera framing must use the new scale together. The dream camera is centred on the entire level and stays fixed while fitting the route in 16:9 and 4:3; visual acceptance of this framing is pending. The ground run is 5 cells/s at Odradek 1 and 4.75 at Odradek 100; a complete dash travels 2.7 cells. A/D changes horizontal speed in the air at 27% of ground acceleration; dash direction is fixed when Shift is pressed. Controlled JumpSpeed is 12.15 local-cell units per second, raising its estimated feet apex from about 2.034 to 2.160 cells for mandatory two-cell rises. Physical route and bypass checks under the new movement remain required.

## Measured on the 32-unit grid

The focused PlayMode results in `Docs/QA/grid-mechanics-summary.csv` and its source XML/logs confirm: L04 player size, movement, fixed camera and laser width; one physical L05 jump onto TimedA (feet apex 2.175 cells, landing centre X=4.704); timed-platform cycling and both L06 movers carrying/resetting; and an L07 wall-jump, contact with button B, jump+dash landing at X=20.443, and bed contact. `grid-mechanism-results.xml` reports 3 passed and 1 skipped case; the skipped L07 mover case is inapplicable because L07 has no mover. These focused tests do not confirm the complete L05 A→B→C→exit chain, the complete L06 route, all 22 contacts, required bypass failures, or final visual seams.

The first independent grid traversal run confirmed the complete L01–L04 and L07 routes (`grid-remaining-results.xml`, 5/5) and four collider/contact checks (`grid-contact-visual-results.xml`, 4/4). Its original `grid-sequence-results.xml` failed the two L05 and two L06 scripted sequences. L05's controlled script jumped from TimedA at X≈142 world units and caught TimedB's left wall at X≈215; responsive landed on TimedC with only 0.04 s remaining under the former 2.8/4 s timer. L06 stayed in contact with its horizontal mover, but the script demanded 256 world units of movement *after* boarding near X≈251, whereas the mover's farthest centre is X=448. That script was changed to stage the takeoffs and check actual arrival. The first seven-dream PlayMode session reached L05 after L01–L04 and died there (`grid-session-results.xml`). All of these L05/L06 results predate the 5.5/7 s timer and Controlled jump update; they do not establish current acceptance.

`grid-bypass-results.xml` includes a sampled L07 route from the solid external cave wall to WallGrabLedge without touching the ledge's own side.
